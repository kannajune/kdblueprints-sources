# AI agents explained: sources and fact check

Video: KD Blueprints Short, October 2026 ([watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/LEdyqvLolW4)).

Every claim in the video, with where it comes from. Facts checked on 9 Oct 2026.

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| OpenAI launched Dots, AI agents inside ChatGPT (DevDay, 29 Sep 2026) | [OpenAI DevDay 2026 recap](https://openai.com/index/devday-2026-recap/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/openai-devday-2026-live-updates.html) |
| Meta launched Muse, a personal AI agent (8 Sep 2026) that "doesn't just answer questions, it actually does the work" | [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/23/tech/meta-muse-ai-agent) |
| You can message Muse on WhatsApp | [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) ("in the Muse app or directly in WhatsApp") |
| Elon Musk's AI company launched Grok Bot, "AI teammates you can give real work to" | [Introducing Grok Bot](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot), [Team Bots (October)](https://x.ai/news/team-bots) |
| Agents can do tasks like booking travel and filling forms | [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) |

Our own illustrations (not claims about any product): the train ticket to Chennai, the agent asking "Shall I pay?" before paying, and the robot characters. None of these companies has said its agent books Indian railway tickets.
