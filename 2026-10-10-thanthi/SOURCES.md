# Thanthi (telegram) Part 1: sources and fact check

Video: KD Blueprints Short, October 2026 (Tamil: [Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/-qxwS2-AR0I), [Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4H8rbOImoWE)).

Facts checked on 10 Oct 2026.

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| India's last telegram was sent on 14 July 2013, ending 163 years of service | [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/last-telegram-ever-to-be-sent-july-14/), [Marginal Revolution](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2013/06/india-to-send-the-worlds-last-telegram-on-july-14th.html) |
| At its peak in 1985, about 60 million telegrams were sent a year | [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/last-telegram-ever-to-be-sent-july-14/) |
| Telegrams were charged by the word | [Farewell to Telegraph Services in India (case study, PDF)](https://www.forum4researchers.com/cw_admin/docs/IJIRP-JULY-13-05.pdf) |

Our own story, not real events: the family in Madras, the midnight knock, the message "FATHER SERIOUS. START IMMEDIATELY." and Chintu the robot.
