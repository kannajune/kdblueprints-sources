What we noticed:
- All 8 spam answers were right, in all three ways.
- The Decisions API answered in about 0.15 to 0.6 seconds, every time.
- Normal chat took 1 to 3 seconds for the first three messages, then 8 to 10 seconds for the rest
  (the servers got slower during our run). So the speed-up was about 5x at chat's best and over 40x at its worst.
- OpenAI says "up to 10x faster". Our run agrees that the Decisions API is much faster;
  how much depends on how busy normal chat is at that moment.
